Official Privacy Policy

Preamble: Our Strong Commitment

At Youth Con-Nect Group of Companies, privacy is the foundation of unity, protection, and mutual respect.

1. Purpose and Scope

This policy governs the collection, use, storage, sharing, protection, retention, and destruction of information across all platforms, communications, financial activities, events, and collaborations.

2. Legal Compliance

Our privacy framework aligns with national and international data protection laws:

  • Uganda’s Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019: Governs personal data processing and protection. Read more
  • General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR - EU): Ensures lawful processing, transparency, and user rights. Learn more
  • African Union Convention on Cyber Security and Personal Data Protection: Strengthens data security across African nations. View details

3. Our Core Principles

  • Honor
  • Trust
  • Transparency
  • Responsibility
  • Protection

4. Information We Collect

We collect:

  • Personal details
  • Identification documents
  • Financial data
  • Educational profiles
  • Emergency contacts
  • Service history

5. Collection and Storage Methods

Information is gathered through:

  • Official forms
  • Secured digital platforms
  • In-person verification

All information is encrypted and securely stored on Youth Con-Nect’s owned infrastructure to comply with security regulations.

6. Lawful Data Processing

Under Uganda’s Data Protection Act, 2019, processing occurs based on:

  • Explicit member consent
  • Contractual obligations
  • Legal requirements
  • Legitimate interest in group administration

7. How We Use Your Information

Collected data supports:

  • Program and service administration
  • Member financial empowerment
  • Leadership development
  • Strategic growth and unity

8. Absolute Internal-Only Handling

All personal data remains strictly internal. Third-party access is prohibited except under extreme necessity with express authorization.

9. Member Rights & Regulatory Reporting

Members have the right to:

  • Access their information
  • Correct inaccuracies
  • Restrict processing
  • Request deletion
  • Object to misuse

If rights are violated, complaints can be filed with Uganda’s Personal Data Protection Office or relevant GDPR regulatory bodies.

10. Confidentiality Obligations

All personnel must:

  • Swear a confidentiality promise
  • Undergo mandatory privacy training
  • Commit to lifetime confidentiality, even post-separation

11. Protection of Children and Vulnerable Persons

Enhanced protections include:

  • Mandatory parental/guardian consent for minors
  • Special safeguards for vulnerable persons

12. Internal Oversight & Data Protection Officer

Youth Con-Nect has a dedicated Data Protection Unit, led by a Data Protection Officer, ensuring compliance and oversight.

13. Secure Digital Infrastructure & Cross-Border Protection

All servers, hosting services, and platforms are fully owned and managed internally for total control and security.

Data processed internationally follows GDPR-approved data transfer mechanisms, including:

  • Standard Contractual Clauses
  • Data Processing Agreements
  • Encryption-based security measures

14. Data Breach Protocol & Notification Compliance

In case of a breach:

  • Threat isolation occurs immediately
  • Affected individuals are notified within 48 hours
  • Regulatory authorities are informed based on Uganda’s legal obligations and GDPR Article 33

15. Secure Data Retention & Destruction

Data is retained based on strict schedules and securely destroyed when no longer needed.

16. Complaints and Appeals

Members may submit privacy complaints internally, with investigations completed within 10 business days.

17. Whistleblower Protection

Full confidentiality and protection for individuals reporting privacy violations or non-compliance.

18. No Third-Party Transfers Clause

Member data is never sold, traded, or leased to any external parties.

19. Updates and Amendments

Policy updates will be communicated transparently, incorporating member feedback where appropriate.

Final Declaration

"United We Stand, Divided We Fall." Youth Con-Nect protects its future by safeguarding every member today. Privacy is the bedrock of our prosperity.
